How a contact moves to Sales

  1. Opens an emailStays in nurture. No sales call yet.
  2. ClicksMarked engaged, retargeted on the topic they clicked.
  3. Downloads or comes backHigh-intent signal, watched closely.
  4. Replies or booksStraight to Sales, prioritised by account.

How I think

A few rules I don't break.

  1. Strategy before creativeAudience, numbers and one clear idea come first. Then the posts.
  2. Success is meetings and pipelineImpressions and likes are signals, not the goal. We agree what counts before we start.
  3. Sales only gets real intentAn open isn't a lead. Replies, bookings and repeat visits are.
  4. No invented claimsNo fake urgency, and no future features sold as live.

How a project runs

  1. Discovery callFree, 30 minutes. Your goals, audience and what's not working.
  2. Audit & strategyWhat to say, where to show up, and the numbers we'll judge it by.
  3. Campaign & assetsThe idea, the plan and every asset, ready to launch.
  4. Launch, measure, improveA clear report, and what to do next.
