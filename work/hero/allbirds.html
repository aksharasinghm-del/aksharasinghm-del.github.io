01 · The challenge

Loved by early adopters. Left behind by everyone else.

Allbirds became famous as the comfortable, sustainable shoe. Then performance brands like On and Hoka took over "comfort" for people on their feet, and Allbirds got filed as a desk shoe.

02 · The insight

Nobody buys shoes to save the planet. They buy them to survive a Tuesday.

So we lead with comfort under pressure and keep sustainability as the proof on every box, not the pitch.

03 · The idea

The 100-Day Shift.

100 people who never sit down wear Allbirds for 100 shifts. Every step is counted. Their worn pairs become the campaign: scuffs, creases and all.

Step 1 · Recruit

100 wearers

Nurses, line cooks, baristas, teachers, dog walkers and new parents in NYC, Chicago and London.

Step 2 · Wear

100 shifts

Step counts and wear diaries, filmed by the wearers themselves. Real, not studio.

Step 3 · Reveal

The scuffs are the ad

Worn pairs shot like museum pieces, then sent to ReRun for a second life.
