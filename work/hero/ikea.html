Hero project 02 · IKEA · DE · FR · NL · ES · UK

Home,
for now.

A 12-month pan-European campaign that turns IKEA's biggest growth audience, young renters, from "I'll furnish it when I own a place" into customers now. Make it yours. Take it with you.

MarketsGermany, France, Netherlands, Spain, UK
AudienceRenters, 18–29

My roleStrategy, products, channel plan, CRM, creative
01 · The challenge

IKEA's next generation of customers don't own their homes, and won't for years.

26.3

years

Average age young Europeans leave home. In Spain it's over 30 (Eurostat, 2023).

~1 in 2

German households rent

Renting is the default life stage, not a short gap before buying (Destatis).

€0

deposit back

if you drill the wrong wall. So many renters don't hang anything at all.

Meet Lena. 26, Berlin, third flat in five years.

What she says

"I'll make it nice when I have a proper place."

What she does

Saves homes on Pinterest. Sleeps on a mattress on the floor. Leaves her furniture on the street when she moves.

Her three blockers

1 · The landlord: no drilling, deposit at risk
2 · The move: "it won't fit the next flat"
3 · The money: furniture feels like sunk cost

Each blocker gets a pillar.

02 · The insight

"For now" lasts years. So renters live like guests in their own homes.

They postpone real furniture to a future that keeps moving. The result: IKEA's biggest growth audience buys the least, not because they don't care, but because it feels like wasted money.

03 · The idea

Home, for now.

Stop treating renting as waiting. IKEA becomes the brand that makes a temporary home feel permanent, and helps it move with you. The campaign isn't an ad. It's a range, a kit and a service.

Pillar 1

Deposit-Safe

A labelled range of freestanding, lean-on and plug-in pieces. In-store tag, online filter, landlord-proof promise.

Solves: the landlord
Pillar 2

Night One

A €199 kit (illustrative): bed, bedding, lamp and kitchen basics, delivered the day you get the keys. Sold with letting agents.

Solves: the first week
Pillar 3

Move-Out Day

Built on IKEA Buy Back & Resell: book a pickup, get store credit, and your piece goes to the next renter.

Solves: the money
04 · The creative system
