Opportunities are my hypotheses from public information. Week 1 of the plan is a full audit with Ugaoo's own analytics to confirm them.

The strategy

From "buy plants" to "we'll keep them alive with you".

The big insight: people don't stop buying plants because they stop liking them. They stop because their last one died. If Ugaoo owns what happens after delivery, every follower stays a customer.

Goal 1: Care

Make Ugaoo the first place people go when a plant looks sad, through a free DM clinic.

Goal 2: Community

Turn customers into content creators with #UgaooGhar, their plant corners at home.

Goal 3: Growth

Reach first-time plant parents with reels built to be shared and saved.

Template rules

  • One idea per post. If it needs two, it's a carousel.
  • Series label top-left, numbered.
  • Always a plant in frame.
  • Hinglish is welcome in headlines, where it sounds natural.
  • Ugaoo wordmark small, bottom-right.

The 90-day plan

Four phases.

Start by listening and setting up systems, then launch the series, then bring in creators and customers, then scale what works.

Days 1–14

Audit and set up

  • Full audit with Ugaoo's own analytics
  • Top 20 posts analysed: what gets saved, shared, DMed
  • Series templates in Canva
  • Clinic DM quick replies and plant-doctor rota
Goal: a baseline and a system
Days 15–45

Launch the series

  • Bhidu-proof plants, weekly
  • Ugaoo Clinic opens in DMs
  • Farm reels from Talegaon
  • Test three hook styles per week
Goal: find what travels
Days 46–75

Bring in the community

  • #UgaooGhar launch: customers' plant corners
  • Seed plants to 25 home and hostel creators
  • Collab posts with café and interior accounts
  • Monthly Clinic Live on Instagram
Goal: customers become creators
Days 76–90

Scale

  • Put paid spend behind the top five reels
  • Gifting season push on Instagram and LinkedIn
  • Broadcast channel drops
  • Report and next-quarter plan
Goal: turn reach into orders

Community rules

  • Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting.
  • Clinic DMs answered within 1 hour, 9am to 9pm.
  • Complaints about deliveries move to DMs with order support, same day.
