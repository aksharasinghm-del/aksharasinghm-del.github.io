What this means for social

Social media became the airport's help desk. People weren't only posting about cancelled flights. They were posting that nobody told them, and that they couldn't find their bags.

So this isn't a "post more ads" problem. It's a trust problem, and trust is built by what the brand says, how fast, and whether it keeps its word.

6E 002 THE INSIGHT

Passengers forgive a delay. They don't forgive being left in the dark.

Stranded travellers reported not being informed about cancellations. Silence is what turns a bad day into a lost customer, so the strategy is built around one habit: tell people first, clearly, and keep telling them.

6E 003 THE STRATEGY

Rebuild trust by being boringly reliable online.

GoalsFaster help, clearer information and visibly better sentiment before the December 2026 peak.
AudienceFrequent flyers, first-time flyers, families travelling for weddings, and business travellers.
Platform rolesX for live service and updates; Instagram for explainers and people stories; LinkedIn for leadership and industry trust; YouTube for behind-the-scenes films.
VoiceCalm, human, specific. Numbers and next steps, never "inconvenience is regretted".
Transparency

Weekly on-time report

Every Monday, the numbers from last week, good or bad. Trust comes from publishing the bad weeks too.

Your rights

Know-your-rights explainers

Cancellations, refunds and lost bags explained in one swipe. People save these before they need them.
