Perception vs reality

The real problem

Bata's problem isn't product or reach. It's that the brand is still judged by a memory from school, and its best new products (Power, Floatz, Hush Puppies) live under separate names, so they don't change how people see Bata itself.

03 Brand architecture

One family name.

Today, Bata's most youthful products sit under separate names, so their success doesn't rub off on Bata. The refresh brings them under one roof.

04 Identity system

The tread.

The signature graphic comes from the one part of a shoe that touches the ground: its tread. Every pattern is a real sole pattern, simplified. It works as a border, a background, a pattern on packaging, or a mask for photos.
